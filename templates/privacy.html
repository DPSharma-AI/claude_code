Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we also store the expenses you log, including amount, category, date, and description. We may collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to operate Spendly, show you your spending summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Spendly database. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.

Third Party Services

We only share data with third parties where needed to run the service, such as hosting providers. These parties may use your data only to provide their services to us.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
